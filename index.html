Legendary Pokémon
Mossdeep Sailor questline: Many legendaries are unlocked through the Sailor with an eye-patch in Mossdeep City (on the hill near the large telescope). His quests can be started after beating the Mossdeep Gym. Speaking to him also unlocks the S.S. Tidal ship.
